About me

I’m Violet, a social media marketer and content creator based in Boca Raton, Florida, and a Business/Marketing student at Florida Atlantic University graduating in December 2026.

I make short-form content that people actually stop scrolling for. My TikTok Shop videos have earned over 8M combined views, including a 2.5M-view video for affordable sets that drove strong sales, and at the FAU Alumni Association, my content was the top performer out of all the student creators, including a post with 1.8M views.

I create across health, fitness, and wellness, fashion, and lifestyle, and I run my own handmade shell jewelry brand, Bottishelli, where I plan and art-direct every shoot myself. From concept and filming to editing and captions, I love bringing a brand’s personality to life.
